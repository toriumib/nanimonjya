Show HN: PetaName — A face & name memory training game built on cognitive science research

Hi HN,

I built PetaName because I kept forgetting people's names at work and it was getting embarrassing.

The app combines a card-matching game with techniques from published memory research: elaborative encoding, the Baker-Baker technique, self-referential processing, spaced retrieval, and the production effect.

What makes it different from other 'brain training' apps:
- You can upload photos of REAL people you need to remember (colleagues, clients, classmates) and the app builds games around them
- A business card mode with OCR, voice introductions, and a meet→delay→recall flow that mirrors real situations
- Every mechanic is traceable to a specific published study (cited in-app)
- No account required. Data stays on-device.

Tech: Flutter + Firebase. Android + Web.
