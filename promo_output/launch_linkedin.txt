After my third time forgetting a colleague's name in the hallway, I decided to fix the problem at the root.

I built PetaName — a face-and-name memory training app based on published cognitive science research.

It uses:
✅ Elaborative encoding (not raw repetition)
✅ The Baker-Baker technique — attach meaning to names
✅ Self-referential processing — connect names to YOUR context
✅ Spaced retrieval with next-day review for wrong answers

You can upload photos of real colleagues, classmates, or clients and the app builds memory games around them.

Free on Android & Web: https://web-sigma-drab-72.vercel.app/

Sales, reception, and client-facing roles share one universal pain point: remembering names.

PetaName has a 'Business Recall' mode that simulates exactly this:
1️⃣ Meet → person hands you a card, introduces themselves (audio)
2️⃣ Delay → a deliberate gap (research shows this strengthens memory)
3️⃣ Recall → face appears. Can you name them? Company? Title?

Wrong answers come back tomorrow (spaced repetition).
You can import real business cards via OCR.

Free on Android & Web: https://web-sigma-drab-72.vercel.app/
