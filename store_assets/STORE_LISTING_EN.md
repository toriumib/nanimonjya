## Full Description (4000 chars max)

```
"Wait... what was their name?" — Never freeze up again.

It's the third time you've met, and you still can't remember their name.
This app was built to erase that awkward moment.
A complete training system for connecting faces to names — backed by memory research.


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
🧑‍🎨 Face Memo — Log real people you need to remember
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

This is the core of the app.
Add coworkers, clients, classmates, friends' family.
Anyone you need to remember goes right into the app.

▼ Build an avatar instead of asking for a photo
You can't always ask someone for their picture.
So we built a face avatar editor — no photo needed.

  • Skin tone / face shape / hairstyle / hair color
  • Eyes / eyebrows / nose / mouth
  • Glasses / beauty marks / facial hair
  • Gender, age range, height cues (changes the full-body look)

"Glasses, long hair, mole on the left cheek" —
choosing features one by one IS the memorization process.
Each feature becomes a tag you can use as a recall cue later.

▼ Photo and business card import
You can also register actual photos.
Snap a business card and the app reads the company name and person's name,
filling in the fields automatically (on-device OCR).

▼ 18 fields per person
Name / reading / nickname / gender / birthday / company / title / occupation /
hometown / address / relationship / phone / email / X / Instagram / LINE ID /
Facebook / free memo

Name alone is fine. You can always fill in more later.

▼ Once registered, they appear in the games
Creating a roster isn't the end.
Study (flashcards), Quiz (4-choice test), Match Play (use this roster in games).
The people you registered become the people you practice with.


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
📣 Name Call — The core memory game
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

One rule:
Someone new appears? Give them a name.
They show up again? Call out their name.
First to answer correctly gets the card.

  • Solo (CPU opponent, 4 difficulty ranks)
  • Pass-and-play (2–4 players on one device)
  • Online (friend match / ranked / turn-based)

Two naming styles:
"Name-as-you-go" — name each person as they appear (default).
"Name-all-first" — name everyone upfront, then the roster is sealed and recall begins.


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
💼 Business Recall — Training for work
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Simulates a real "nice to meet you" interaction.

  ① Meet — someone hands you a business card and introduces themselves (with voice)
  ② Delay — a deliberate gap passes (memory tips shown during the wait)
  ③ Recall — see the face, answer: name, company, title, phone, email

Choose which fields to be quizzed on.
People you got wrong come back the next day (spaced review).


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
🚀 Project Noah — A sci-fi story where memory shapes the ending
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

A distant future. The sun is expanding.
You board a ship leaving Earth, bound for a star 49 light-years away.

People you meet hand you their business cards — name, affiliation, hobbies, contact.
You see each one only once.
After 330 years of cryosleep, how many can you still remember?
How many you recall determines who makes it with you.

The more you remember, the more people come along.
A story mode where memorization IS the gameplay.


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
📚 Reading — Why can't we remember names?
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

25+ articles distilling memory research into practical name-remembering techniques.
Every article cites its sources: author, year, journal.

  • Retrieval practice beats re-reading for long-term retention
    (Roediger & Karpicke, 2006, Psychological Science)
  • Why names are uniquely hard to remember — the Baker/baker paradox
    (McWeeny, Young, Hay & Ellis, 1987, British Journal of Psychology)
  • Self-referential processing boosts memory
    (Rogers, Kuiper & Kirker, 1977, J. Personality & Social Psychology)
  • Saying it out loud — the production effect
    (MacLeod et al., 2010, J. Exp. Psychol.: LMC)
  • Sleep and memory consolidation
    (Staresina, 2024, Trends in Cognitive Sciences)

The game mechanics are built on these findings.
The deliberate delay in Business Recall. The next-day review for wrong answers.
The recall-over-recognition design. It all comes from here.

※ The research cited is about memory and learning in general.
   It is not research conducted on this specific app.


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Who is this for?
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  • You just started a new job or moved to a new team — too many names at once
  • You work in sales, service, or reception — need to remember client names
  • You're starting a new school year — need to learn classmates' names
  • You've noticed names slipping more often lately
  • You want to remember relatives' and friends' family names
```
