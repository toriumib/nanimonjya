### 説明欄（本文）

```
You recognise the face. The name is gone. That is not a bad memory — names are the one
thing about a person that means nothing, and human memory runs on meaning.

PetaName is a free face-and-name memory game built around that problem. A new face
appears, you give it a name, and a little later that same face comes back with no name
on it. Say it first and you take the card.

▶ Play free in your browser (no install):
https://web-sigma-drab-72.vercel.app

▶ Android:
https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.nanimonjya

── What is in the app ──
• Name Call — name each new face, recall it when it returns
• Party mode — 2 to 4 players passing one phone
• Card Memory — people introduce themselves with a business card, then you try to
  remember who was who after a delay
• Face Notes — add photos of real colleagues and classmates and practise on them
• Spaced review — anyone you get wrong comes back on a later day (1, 3, 7, 14, 30)

── Why it is built this way ──
Testing yourself beats rereading, and recalling something after a gap beats cramming.
Those two findings shaped the whole app: the quiz always makes you produce the name
rather than recognise it, and people you missed come back on later days instead of
immediately.

Guides on how to remember names, written from the research:
https://web-sigma-drab-72.vercel.app/learn/

── Notes ──
The faces in this video are not real people. They are generated images (StyleGAN2,
Karras et al.), used so that nobody's likeness is involved.

This is a game for practising a specific skill — linking faces to names. It is not a
medical product, and it has not been shown to prevent or treat any condition.

#memorygame #rememberingnames #braintraining #indiegame #flutter
```
