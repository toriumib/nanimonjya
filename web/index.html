Namaegao: Never Forget a Name Again

Namaegao is a free face-and-name memory training game for Android and Web. Match face cards with name cards to train your memory — backed by cognitive science techniques used by memory champions.

Why Namaegao?

Based on Science
Uses elaborative encoding, the Baker-Baker technique, and spaced retrieval — the same methods proven in memory research (Craik & Tulving 1975, McWeeny 1987).
Real Face Photos
Train with realistic face images, not cartoons. Practice in conditions closer to real-life name recall.
Multiple Game Modes
Name Call (name recall race), Pair Matching (classic memory), Business Training (meet → delay → recall), and more.
Play Together
Local multiplayer (2–4 players on one device), online ranked matches, and CPU opponents with ranking system.
Upload Your Own
Add real people you need to remember — colleagues, classmates, new team members. Practice with their photos and names.
Free to Play
No paywall. Optional extras: unlock bonus characters, remove ads, or equip lucky charms with coin earnings.

ペタネーム — 顔と名前の記憶トレーニング

ペタネームは、顔と名前を結びつける記憶力を楽しく鍛える無料ゲームです。 認知科学の研究成果に基づいた記憶術（タグ付け法・ベイカー錯誤の逆用・自己関連づけ・分散検索）を ゲームに取り入れ、職場や学校での「顔はわかるのに名前が出てこない」を克服します。

モード：なまえコール（名前想起レース）／ペアさがし（神経衰弱）／ビジネス特訓（出会い→時間→思い出し）／ おぼえる（自分の写真で練習）／オンライン対戦／CPU対戦（段位制）
